Yonsei University Researchers Visit Science Tokyo for Collaborative Research on LLM-Based Semantic Communications

June 21 – September 18, 2026 Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan

Hyelin Nam and Jinhyuk Kim, Ph.D. students from Yonsei University, South Korea, visited the Sakaguchi Laboratory at the Institute of Science Tokyo from June 21 to September 18, 2026, for collaborative research on LLM-based semantic communications.

From June 21 to September 18, 2026, Hyelin Nam and Jinhyuk Kim, Ph.D. students from Yonsei University, South Korea, visited the Sakaguchi Laboratory at the Institute of Science Tokyo for a research exchange focusing on large language model (LLM)-based semantic communications.

Their research interests span generative AI, semantic communications, AI-native radio access networks (RAN), O-RAN, and edge intelligence. During their visit, they explored potential applications of LLMs to intelligent next-generation wireless communication systems, further strengthening research collaboration between Yonsei University and Science Tokyo.
