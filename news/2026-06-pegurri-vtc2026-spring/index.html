ASPIRE real-time V2X digital twin results presented at IEEE VTC2026-Spring

Jun 10, 2026 IEEE VTC2026-Spring, Nice, France

On June 10, 2026, Roberto Pegurri presented the ASPIRE collaboration's joint research at the IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2026-Spring) in Nice, France, with the paper "Predicting Networks Before They Happen: Experimentation on a Real-Time V2X Digital Twin."

On June 10, 2026, Roberto Pegurri — a PhD student from Polytechnic University of Milan (Prof. Antonio Capone's group) — presented joint work from the ASPIRE collaboration at the IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2026-Spring), held in Nice, France.

Predicting Networks Before They Happen: Experimentation on a Real-Time V2X Digital Twin

R. Pegurri, S. Habu, F. Linsalata, K. Wang, T. Yu, E. Moro, M. Igarashi, A. Capone, and K. Sakaguchi

IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2026-Spring), Nice, France, June 2026
