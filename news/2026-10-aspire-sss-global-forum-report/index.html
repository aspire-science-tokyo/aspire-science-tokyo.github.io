ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026 Held to Promote International Research Collaboration

October 26–29, 2026Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan

Science Tokyo hosted the ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026 on October 26–29, bringing together international researchers, industry representatives, students, and early-career researchers to advance digital twin collaboration.

  • event
  • digital twin
  • collaboration

Institute of Science Tokyo (Science Tokyo) hosted the ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026 from October 26 to 29, 2026, jointly organized by the JST ASPIRE Program and the Academy for Super Smart Society (WISE-SSS).

Under the theme “Real-Time Digital Twin Platforms for a Super Smart Society,” the forum brought together more than 100 participants, including researchers from international partner universities and research institutions, industry representatives, early-career researchers, and students.

The four-day program featured Special Sessions in different research fields, research facility tours, Keynote Speeches by international researchers, an Interactive Session featuring digital poster presentations by students and early-career researchers, and Networking Sessions. These activities provided opportunities to share research achievements, strengthen interdisciplinary collaboration, and promote international joint research on digital twin technologies.

Special Sessions on Food & Agriculture and Manufacturing (October 26, Yokohama Campus)

On October 26, the forum began at Yokohama Campus with the Smart Agriculture Tour and a tour of the TSUBAME4.0 supercomputer facilities.

During the Smart Agriculture Tour, participants visited the Smart Agriculture Education and Research Field, where research activities involving sensing, wireless communications, robotics, and digital twin technologies for agricultural applications were introduced. The TSUBAME4.0 Tour provided an opportunity to learn about the high-performance computing infrastructure supporting large-scale simulations and AI research.

In the afternoon, the Food & Agriculture and Manufacturing Special Sessions were held. Researchers from Japan and overseas presented ongoing research activities and discussed digital twin use cases, technical challenges, and future directions for international research collaboration in these fields.

Special Session on Healthcare (October 27, Yushima Campus)

On October 27, the Healthcare Special Session and research facility tours were held at Yushima Campus.

Participants visited research facilities related to dysphagia rehabilitation and robotics, where research activities and advanced technologies for healthcare, nursing, and rehabilitation were introduced.

The Healthcare Special Session provided opportunities to exchange research findings and discuss the application of digital twin technologies to healthcare, as well as potential areas for future international collaboration.

The ASPIRE General Assembly was also held to share the overall progress of the project and discuss future research activities and collaboration among participating institutions.

1 / 4

Main Event: Keynote Speeches and Interactive Session (October 28, Ookayama Campus)

The Main Event was held on October 28 at West Building 9, Ookayama Campus, bringing together researchers, students, and industry representatives from Japan and overseas. The event began with Opening Remarks and an introduction to the JST ASPIRE Program and WISE-SSS.

Keynote Speeches

The Keynote Speeches featured nine invited researchers from universities and research institutions in Japan and overseas. Three keynote talks were delivered during Keynote Speeches I in the morning, followed by six talks during Keynote Speeches II in the afternoon. The speakers presented their latest research achievements and future perspectives, followed by questions and discussions on technical challenges and opportunities for international research collaboration.

1 / 11

Interactive Session

The Interactive Session featured Digital Poster Presentations by students and early-career researchers. The session consisted of two 45-minute rotations, providing opportunities for participants to present their research findings and engage in active discussions across different research fields and institutions.

1 / 4

Networking Session

The Main Event concluded with Closing Remarks and a Group Photo, followed by a Networking Session, where researchers, students, and industry representatives further strengthened their professional connections.

Through the Keynote Speeches, Interactive Session, and Networking Session, the Main Event provided valuable opportunities to exchange research findings, promote international collaboration, and support the development of early-career researchers.

1 / 4

Special Session on Mobility and AI & Digital Twin (October 29, Ishikawadai Area)

On October 29, the final day of the forum, the Mobility and AI & Digital Twin Special Sessions and research facility tours were held in the Ishikawadai area.

During the facility tour, participants were introduced to research activities conducted by the Shino and Yoshitake Laboratories and observed a demonstration of a driving simulator. The visit provided insights into experimental approaches and the application of digital twin technologies in mobility research.

The Mobility Special Session focused on digital twin use cases, technical challenges, and opportunities for international collaboration in mobility research.

The AI & Digital Twin Special Session addressed the theme of an AI-native Digital Twin Platform, with discussions on technical challenges and future research directions for integrating artificial intelligence and digital twin technologies.

Summary and Future Perspectives

The ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026 provided valuable opportunities for researchers from different fields and countries to share research achievements, explore technical challenges, and strengthen interdisciplinary and international collaboration.

By combining Special Sessions, research facility tours, Keynote Speeches, and Interactive Sessions, the forum encouraged discussions connecting digital twin research with real-world applications in agriculture, manufacturing, healthcare, and mobility.

Through continued activities under the JST ASPIRE Program and WISE-SSS, we aim to further promote international joint research, support the international activities and development of early-career researchers, and contribute to the realization of a super smart society.
