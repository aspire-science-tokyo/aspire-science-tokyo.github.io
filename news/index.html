June 2026 News

Prof. Kei Sakaguchi visits RWTH Aachen's ika to explore mobility research collaboration

In June 2026, Prof. Kei Sakaguchi visited the Institute for Automotive Engineering (ika) at RWTH Aachen University and toured their mobility research, with both groups looking forward to collaboration within the ASPIRE framework.

Jun 10, 2026 News

ASPIRE real-time V2X digital twin results presented at IEEE VTC2026-Spring

On June 10, 2026, Roberto Pegurri presented the ASPIRE collaboration's joint research at IEEE VTC2026-Spring in Nice, France, with the paper "Predicting Networks Before They Happen: Experimentation on a Real-Time V2X Digital Twin."

Apr 15, 2026 News

Polytechnic University of Milan researcher visits Science Tokyo for joint V2X digital twin experiment

Roberto Pegurri, a PhD student from Polytechnic University of Milan (Prof. Antonio Capone's group), visited Institute of Science Tokyo from April 6 to April 15, 2026 to join a joint proof-of-concept experiment on real-time digital twin based V2X topology dynamic control with the Sakaguchi Lab.
