Virginia Tech Researcher Paris Karakasis Visits Science Tokyo for Joint Research on mmWave Beam and Blockage Prediction

June 25 – July 30, 2026 Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan

Paris Karakasis, a Postdoctoral Associate at Virginia Tech, visited the Sakaguchi Laboratory at the Institute of Science Tokyo from June 25 to July 30, 2026, to conduct collaborative research on sensor-robust mmWave beam and blockage prediction.

From June 25 to July 30, 2026, Paris Karakasis, a Postdoctoral Associate at the Institute for Advanced Computing, Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University (Virginia Tech), visited the Sakaguchi Laboratory at the Institute of Science Tokyo as part of an international research exchange.

During his visit, he conducted collaborative research on “Task-Oriented Structured Fusion for Sensor-Robust mmWave Beam and Blockage Prediction.” The research explores multimodal sensor fusion approaches for predicting millimeter-wave (mmWave) beam conditions and communication blockage, with an emphasis on robustness to sensor-related uncertainties.

His research interests include signal processing, multimodal learning, optimization, tensor decomposition, and graph mining.

The visit provided an opportunity to exchange research expertise and explore potential directions for future collaboration in intelligent wireless communication systems.
