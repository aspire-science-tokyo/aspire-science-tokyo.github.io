ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026: Real-Time Digital Twin Platforms for a Super Smart Society

October 26–29, 2026Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan

The ASPIRE Program and WISE-SSS will jointly host the ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum and Special Sessions from October 26 to 29, 2026, across the Ookayama, Yokohama, and Yushima campuses of Science Tokyo.

  • event
  • digital twin
  • collaboration

The ASPIRE Program and the Academy for Super Smart Society (WISE-SSS) at the Institute of Science Tokyo will jointly host the ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026 from October 26 to 29, 2026, under the theme “Real-Time Digital Twin Platforms for a Super Smart Society.” International research partners, industry participants, students, and early-career researchers will come together for keynote speeches, interactive presentations, field-specific special sessions, site visits, and networking.

Event Information

Dates: October 26–29, 2026
Locations: Ookayama, Yokohama, and Yushima campuses, Institute of Science Tokyo
Main event: Wednesday, October 28, Ookayama Campus (West Building 9)
Language: English
Eligible participants: ASPIRE researchers and students, WISE-SSS students, Science Tokyo faculty, and SSS Promotion Consortium partners.

Program at a Glance

The main forum takes place on October 28; special sessions and lab tours on other days are optional. Times and items marked TBA or TBC are subject to change.

Presentations

WISE-SSS students are required to attend the October 28 sessions. Students who have not presented at a previous Global Forum are requested to present in Interactive Session (E). ASPIRE students and early-career researchers are requested to deliver a pitch presentation in one of the designated Special Sessions. Instructions for presentation material submission will be provided to registered presenters.

Registration and Contact

Please register online by October 16, 2026. Registration may close earlier if capacity is reached. Please select the sessions you plan to attend or present in when completing the form.
