ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum 2026: Real-Time Digital Twin Platforms for a Super Smart Society

October 26–29, 2026 Institute of Science Tokyo (Ookayama, Yokohama, and Yushima campuses)

The ASPIRE Program and the Academy for Super Smart Society (WISE-SSS) at Science Tokyo will jointly host the ASPIRE × SSS Global Forum and Special Sessions in October 2026, bringing together researchers from overseas partner institutions, industry members, students, and early-career researchers.

  • event
  • global forum
  • collaboration

The forum combines keynote speeches by leading international researchers, an interactive poster session, field-specific Special Sessions with site visits, and a cross-disciplinary networking session, with the aim of fostering international joint research and new collaborations.

Key Information

  • Dates: Monday, October 26 – Thursday, October 29, 2026
  • Venue: Institute of Science Tokyo (Ookayama, Yokohama, and Yushima campuses)
  • Main Event: Wednesday, October 28, Ookayama Campus
  • Language: English
  • Eligible participants: Researchers and students participating in this ASPIRE project; WISE-SSS students; Science Tokyo faculty; and SSS Promotion Consortium partners
  • Registration deadline: October 16, 2026 (registration closes early if capacity is reached)
