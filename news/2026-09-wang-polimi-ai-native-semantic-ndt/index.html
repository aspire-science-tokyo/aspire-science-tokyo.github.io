Science Tokyo Researcher Dr. Kui Wang Visits Politecnico di Milano for Collaborative Research on AI-Native Semantic Network Digital Twins

September 28 – October 10, 2026Politecnico di Milano, Italy

Dr. Kui Wang from the Institute of Science Tokyo visited Politecnico di Milano, Italy, from September 28 to October 10, 2026, to conduct collaborative research on AI-native semantic network digital twins (NDTs).

From September 28 to October 10, 2026, Dr. Kui Wang from the Institute of Science Tokyo visited Politecnico di Milano, Italy, for collaborative research on AI-native semantic network digital twins (NDTs).

The research exchange focused on exploring how artificial intelligence and semantic approaches can be integrated into network digital twin frameworks to support intelligent network modeling and management.

The visit provided an opportunity for research discussions and further strengthened collaboration between the Institute of Science Tokyo and Politecnico di Milano under the ASPIRE framework.
