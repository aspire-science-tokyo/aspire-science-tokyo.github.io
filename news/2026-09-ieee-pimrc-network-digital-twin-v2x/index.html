ASPIRE Research on Network Digital Twin-Based mmWave V2X Channel Prediction Presented at IEEE PIMRC 2026

September 1–4, 2026Singapore

Shintaro Habu presented ASPIRE research on network digital twin-based channel prediction for millimeter-wave V2X communications at IEEE PIMRC 2026, held in Singapore from September 1 to 4, 2026.

  • conference
  • mobility
  • digital twin

From September 1 to 4, 2026, Shintaro Habu participated in the IEEE International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications (PIMRC 2026) in Singapore, where he presented the research titled “Proof-of-Concept of Network Digital Twin-Based Channel Prediction for mmWave V2X Communications.”

The research explores the use of a network digital twin to predict wireless channel conditions for millimeter-wave vehicle-to-everything (mmWave V2X) communications, supporting the development of more predictive and adaptive connected mobility systems.

The presentation offered an opportunity to share the ASPIRE research approach with the international wireless communications community and exchange ideas on digital twin-enabled vehicular networking.
