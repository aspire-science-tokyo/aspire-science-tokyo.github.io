Yushima Young Researcher Meeting Brings Together Researchers Across Mobility, Food, Nursing, and Digital Twin

August 4, 2026 Yushima Campus, Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan

The Yushima Young Researcher Meeting was held on August 4, 2026, at the Yushima Campus of the Institute of Science Tokyo, bringing together researchers for interdisciplinary discussions, research presentations, a robotics laboratory tour, and networking activities.

  • event
  • young researchers
  • collaboration

On August 4, 2026, the Yushima Young Researcher Meeting was held at the Yushima Campus of the Institute of Science Tokyo, providing an opportunity for young researchers to present their work, exchange ideas, and explore interdisciplinary collaboration.

The meeting featured four special sessions covering Mobility, Food, Nursing, and Digital Twin. Researchers from different fields shared their latest research activities and discussed potential opportunities for collaboration. The Digital Twin session included presentations by Hyelin Nam and Jinhyuk Kim, visiting Ph.D. students from Yonsei University, South Korea.

Following the research sessions, participants joined a robotics laboratory tour led by Genki Kanda. The event concluded with a social gathering, offering further opportunities for networking and informal discussions.

The meeting strengthened connections among young researchers and encouraged future interdisciplinary research collaboration.
