International exchange

International exchange & collaboration

The aim is to connect platform work, domain questions, and institutional collaboration in a form that supports sustained academic exchange and young researcher cultivation.

Main activities

International exchange is an integral part of the project. The platform is developed through sustained collaboration among partner institutions in Japan and overseas, combining joint research, forums, matching workshops, and researcher exchange. These activities help connect field-driven challenges with platform development, form international research teams, and accelerate the circulation of knowledge across domains, institutions, and countries.

Forum

A shared venue for comparing cross-domain findings and shaping common research questions.

Workshop

Small-group sessions for identifying themes, methods, and possible joint work.

Exchange

Research visits and collaborative activity that move methods and working knowledge across institutions.

How to participate

If you would like to apply international exchange, or discuss colaboration, use the shared contact point.
